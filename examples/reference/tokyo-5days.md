### Trip-specific notes
- Japan is **visa-free** for Taiwan passport holders for stays ≤ 90 days. No visa application needed.
- No vaccinations required for entry from Taiwan.
- Travel advisory level for Tokyo: standard precautions (low risk).
- **Customs**: declare any medication with prescription; some OTC drugs (e.g., pseudoephedrine) are restricted.
- **Tipping**: not customary. Don't leave coins on the table.
- **Shoes off**: required at temples, traditional restaurants, some museums. Slip-ons save time.
- **Toilets**: most public toilets have both Western and Japanese styles; bidet controls on the right side typically.
- **Common scams**: rare in Tokyo; watch for overcharging at tourist-area restaurants with no menu posted.
- Emergency numbers: **110** (police), **119** (ambulance / fire), **+81-3-3280-7811** (Taiwan representative office).

### Clothing recommendations
- **Day**: long-sleeve shirt or light sweater; comfortable walking pants or jeans.
- **Evening**: light jacket (temps drop to ~15°C); layers recommended.
- **Footwear**: comfortable walking shoes — expect 10–15 km/day.
- **Rain**: foldable umbrella; packable rain jacket (cheap and good at Don Quijote if needed).

- **Cultural**: avoid overly casual wear at nicer restaurants; smart casual is fine.
